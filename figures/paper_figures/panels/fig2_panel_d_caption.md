# Figure 2d caption stub (tree of RTE copies, both species)

**Shown.** Circular phylogram of 716 RTE core copies (316 C. macrocephalus, 400 C. gariepinus), branch lengths in substitutions/site (scale bar 0.05). Branches are coloured by species where every copy below is one species (green Mac, plum Gar), grey where mixed. Inner ring = species of the copy; outer ring = position of the copy: inside a centromere of the GarSat03 / MacSat03 group (the RTE-carrying centromeric families; crimson; 212 Mac + 28 Gar), inside a GarSat04 or 5S rDNA-array (GarSat49 / MacSat06) centromere (blue; 8 Gar), inside a centromere of another family (olive; 2 Gar), outside the centromere (pale grey; 104 Mac + 362 Gar). Dots = nodes with SH-aLRT >= 80 (209 drawn). Three terminal branches longer than 0.225 (longest 0.309 from the midpoint) are cut at 0.225 (white break).

**Rooting.** The tree file is UNROOTED (IQ-TREE, no outgroup; trifurcation at the file root). It is drawn from the midpoint of the longest tip-to-tip path (0.618), for display only; support stays on its branch. No root is claimed. Parsimony and nearest-neighbour statistics do not depend on the root.

**Verdict (not in the picture).** Copies fall into species-specific clades; the species do not interleave. Nearest tip in the other species: 3.6 % observed vs 49.3 % with species labels shuffled (95 % range 44.7-53.9 %; 1,000 permutations, seed 1; p <= 0.001; Mac copies 1.9 %, Gar copies 5.0 %). Minimum species changes on the tree (parsimony): 15 vs 233.7 shuffled (220-247); 17 vs 265.5 with branches SH-aLRT < 80 collapsed. Arm copies only (104 Mac + 362 Gar, so the result is not carried by homogenised centromeric copies): nearest tip other species 4.7 % vs 34.9 %; parsimony 14 vs 94.6. Centromeric copies only (212 Mac + 38 Gar): 1.6 % vs 25.9 %; parsimony 4 vs 36.2. Association index 2.90 vs 34.97; internal nodes with both species 8.7 % vs 80.1 %. Maximal single-species clades >= 10 copies at SH-aLRT >= 90: Gar 264 (+34, 14, 14), Mac 241; 241 of 316 Mac and 326 of 400 Gar copies lie in them; 14 mixed clades of <= 100 tips at SH-aLRT >= 80 (largest 55 tips = 37 Mac + 18 Gar). Median patristic distance: Gar-Gar 0.104, Mac-Mac 0.169, Gar-Mac 0.183. Reading: consistent with an element present before the species split whose copies seen today expanded separately in each species; it does not date either expansion.

**Interleaving test and null.** Species labels shuffled over the tips of the same tree (1,000 permutations); statistics: nearest-tip other-species share, minimum parsimony changes (Sankoff), association index, share of mixed internal nodes; observed lower than null = species structure. In this module the observed NN share (0.03631) and parsimony (15) were recomputed from the tree and match panel_d_stats.tsv; the null values are read from that table (permutations not re-run).

**Evidence level.** L1 (sequence distances; no dating).

**Sources.** LTR_MacSat03_2026-10-22/tree/copies.treefile (IQ-TREE -fast, SH-aLRT 1,000); positions from the tip labels (centromere interval +-1 Mb, v4.5.3); fig2_panel_d_numbers.tsv and fig2_panel_d_stats_full.tsv (= panel_d_stats.tsv).

**Terminology (2026-11-01).** The tip categories are those written into the tip labels by panels_rte/panel_d.py (top family of the v4.5.3 centromere table: GarSat03/09/10, MacSat03/51/162 = GarSat03 / MacSat03 group; GarSat04/49, MacSat06 = GarSat04 / 5S rDNA arrays). The word 'lineage' is dropped (SatChain v4.7); the tree, positions and null are unchanged.

**Caveats.** Stratified subset of 316 + 400 copies (not the 1,560-copy tree; the UFBoot run crashed earlier and was not repeated); the tree is unrooted (midpoint display only); centromere intervals v4.5.3, not v4.6; no calibrated rate or clock; the 28 Gar copies in GarSat03 / MacSat03-group centromeres in the subset differ from the 77 of panel c (v4.7 intervals) (subset vs all 1,244 copies). The largest Gar clade is 264 copies here vs 266 in the earlier repo README (Mac 241 identical); cause not chased (likely support handling at the re-rooted branch), no effect on any test.
