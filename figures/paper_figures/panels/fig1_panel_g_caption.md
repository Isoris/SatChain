**Sweep (72 scoring sets: match 1-3, mismatch -1 to -4, gap open -3/-5/-8, gap extend -1/-2).** Segment length is NOT stable: it falls into two classes. GarSat04: 43-50 bp core in 21 of 72 sets (stringent), 89-122 bp in the other 51; never 60-80 bp (median 96 bp, 90 bp over the 54 sets whose break-even identity mismatch/(match+mismatch) is >= 50%; the sets below 50% extend into random sequence). MacSat06: 10-47 bp in 23 sets, 74 bp in 6 (strict gap penalties), 96-130 bp in 43 (median 110 bp). The 5' end of the gene (nt 1-7) is not matched in GarSat04 in any set. The centromeric controls give a 7-22 bp hit (indistinguishable from shuffles) in the default set; its longer hits in lenient sets are noise (median identity 65%, down to 47%).

**Copies agree.** 200 random genomic monomers per family (copies_check.tsv): GarSat04 median 81 bp (IQR 81-82), 80% identity, 96.5% with >= 60 bp, 9% in the 60-80 bp window; MacSat06 median 110 bp (IQR 109-114), 83% identity, 100% >= 60 bp, 0.5% in 60-80 bp; GarSat03 / MacSat03 aligned 13-16 bp (score <= 24, within the shuffle range). The 16 individual 5S genes against the consensus give GarSat04 86-94 bp (identity 77-81%) and MacSat06 77-115 bp (70-80%). The v4.5 consensuses reproduce this (GarSat04 76-82 bp at 79-83%, MacSat06 97-115 bp at 79-83%).

**Verdict on the "60-80 bp 5S-related segment" statement.** Not supported as a range. The data support a segment of about 80-90 bp (GarSat04: 81 bp in genomic copies, 89 bp in the consensus) to about 110 bp (MacSat06), i.e. 70-96% of the 115-bp gene at 71-83% identity, always above the shuffled null. "60-80 bp" is correct only as a lower bound for GarSat04 genomic copies (81 bp) and as the stringent-scoring core; only 9% (GarSat04) and 0.5% (MacSat06) of copies and 0 of 72 (GarSat04) / 6 of 72 (MacSat06) scoring sets give 60-80 bp. Suggested wording: "an 80-110 bp segment (70-96% of the 115-bp 5S gene) at 71-83% identity". GarSat49 is a complete 5S rDNA unit (gene 100% identical plus ~94 bp spacer), not a satellite that carries a fragment; SatChain v4.7 classes it, like MacSat06, as a 5S rDNA gene array.
