# Figure 2c caption stub (RTE core copies inside / outside the centromere) - SatChain v4.7, 2026-11-01

**Shown.** RTE core copies (RT-bearing core, positions 5,560-7,400 of the element, 1,841 bp; a copy = chained BLAST hits covering >= 70 % of the core, span <= 1.5 x core) of the two haplotypes, as 100 % stacked bars per species and family group of the centromere: cen = the GarSat03 / MacSat03 group (the RTE-carrying centromeric families, v4.7 sequence cluster C2.1; reds), 5S = GarSat04 (the 5S-derived satellite, cluster C2.2; blues), other = any other dominant family, a gene array (the MacSat06 5S rDNA array on Mac LG22, the GarSat49 array on Gar LG04) or no v4.7 rows (olive). Dark = copy inside the centromere interval +-1 Mb, pale = outside; counts and % are printed on the bars. Table rows: n chr. = chromosomes whose centromere falls in that group; copies = core copies on those chromosomes; % near cen = share of copies within 5 kb of a run of a C2.1 family. Small multiples: the same "% inside" for window +-0.5 / 1 / 2 Mb; marker = reference definition (>= 70 % core coverage; circle Mac, square Gar), bar = range over copy definitions covering >= 50 / 70 / 90 % of the core. The word "lineage" is dropped.

**Numbers (reference setting, +-1 Mb, >= 70 %; v4.7 intervals).** Mac cen 208 of 293 inside (71 %, 22 chr: all but LG10, LG14, LG17, LG18, LG22), Gar cen 77 of 509 (15 %, 13 chr), Gar 5S 12 of 503 (2 %, 9 chr: LG01, 02, 05, 07, 09, 14, 15, 16, 18), Mac other 4 of 23 (17 %, 5 chr), Gar other 21 of 232 (9 %, 6 chr: LG04, 13, 17, 23, 27, 28). All Mac 212 of 316 (67 %), all Gar 110 of 1,244 (9 %). % near cen: 63 / 4 / 6 / 0 / 6. There is no "Mac 5S" bar: in v4.7 no *C. macrocephalus* centromere has a C2.2 family as its dominant family (MacSat06 is a gene array; the v4.6 version drew "Mac B" = 2 chromosomes, 37 of 49 copies inside, from MacSat06-topped centromeres).

**Evidence level.** L1 (sequence, in silico).
