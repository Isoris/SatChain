# Fig 1C caption stub (2026-11-01, SatChain v4.7 wording)

**c | Reference centromeres: composition of every centromere of the assembly in both haplotypes.** Per chromosome (number above the column), from the first centromeric satellite run (y = 0, Mb; the 150 kb before it are shown as negative values), left to right: strand strip (copy-level strand blocks, majority / minority strand), satellite runs by family on a pale-grey centromere background, transposable-element strip, homogenised-core bars (h >= 0.5 and h >= 0.3, W = 20 kb, k = 21), assembly gaps (dotted). Gene arrays (5S rDNA, tRNA, U snRNA) are hatched gold: the *C. macrocephalus* 5S rDNA array MacSat06 (Mac LG01 top, LG20, LG22) and the *C. gariepinus* 5S rDNA array GarSat49 (Gar LG04, LG13) are gene arrays in SatChain v4.7, not satellite families, and are drawn as such. The thin bar above each column is the cue for the main family of the centromere: dark red = GarSat03 / MacSat03 group (v4.7 sequence cluster C2.1), navy = GarSat04 (cluster C2.2, the 5S-derived satellite), gold = gene arrays dominate (> 50 % of the satellite bp). Evidence level L1 (coordinates).

**Numbers (fig1_panel_c_numbers.tsv).** 27 Mac + 27 Gar centromere regions (the regions, runs, strand blocks, TE features and cores are the 2026-10-24 composition tables, read in place). Main family: Mac MacSat03 on 22, MacSat06 (5S rDNA array) on 2 (LG01, LG22), MacSat161 (gene array) 1, MacSat1272 1 (LG20), MacSat162 1 (LG11); Gar GarSat03 10, GarSat04 10, GarSat09 2, GarSat49 (5S rDNA array) 2, GarSat91 1 (LG24), GarSat284 1, GarSat156 1 (LG28, cluster unassigned). Gene-array bp inside the regions: 1,188 kb (was 517 kb when MacSat06 / GarSat49 counted as satellites). Cluster of the main family: C2.1 on 38 regions, C2.2 on 10, gene array on 5, unassigned 1 (the 2026-10-27 version read 'lineage A' 39 / 'lineage B' 14 / unresolved 1 from the v4.6 column).

**What changed on 2026-11-01.** Family ids and names come from the v4.7 catalogue (`cat59/59_catalogue_{Mac,Gar}.tsv`, name_v46 -> family) and nomenclature (`runs_of_record/satellite_family_nomenclature.tsv`: element_class, seq_cluster); the v4.6 manuscript codes of the composition tables that v4.7 renumbered (e.g. CmaSat002-214 = MacSat06, CmaSat011-215 = MacSat162) are translated through the v4.6 nomenclature as a last fallback only. The v4.6 'lineage' column is no longer used. The composition tables cover all chromosomes, including Mac LG14 / 17 / 18 and Gar LG23 / 27 / 28, which have no rows in the v4.7 runs of record (a v4.7 data gap); their columns are drawn from the 2026-10-24 tables.
