**Figure 1 | Genomic organisation of the satellite DNA families of the F₁ hybrid catfish.** Two groups of families are shown throughout: the 5S-related families, i.e. GarSat04 (a satellite derived from the 5S rRNA gene; SatChain v4.7 sequence cluster C2.2, the only family that separates from the others in the k-mer clustering of centromeric monomers) together with the 5S rDNA gene arrays of the two subgenomes (GarSat49 in *Clarias gariepinus*, MacSat06 in *C. macrocephalus*; gene arrays, not satellite families), and the centromeric families, i.e. the GarSat03 / MacSat03 group (GarSat03, GarSat09, GarSat10, MacSat03, MacSat162; sequence cluster C2.1), the RTE-carrying centromeric families. **(A)** Phylogenetic relationships among monomers of the 5S-related families (left) and of the centromeric families (right) in the two subgenomes. Tips are coloured by family, with symbols distinguishing species, 5S rRNA gene references and branch support (ultrafast bootstrap, UFBoot). Outer rings indicate the chromosome carrying each array, its species of origin and its centromeric or non-centromeric location (SatChain v4.7 runs of record). Clades S1–S6 and R1–R19 are defined by a swept patristic-diameter cut. Scale bars represent 0.05 substitutions per site. **(B)** Nucleotide alignments of consecutive monomers from one representative run of each family (the most homogeneous of the five largest v4.7 runs): GarSat03 and MacSat03 (centromeric), GarSat04 (5S-derived satellite) and MacSat06 (5S rDNA array). Rows represent consecutive monomers and columns aligned nucleotide positions; colours distinguish A, C, G and T. Monomer lengths and sequence identities to the corresponding consensus are indicated. Boxes delimit the 5S-related segment (81 bp in GarSat04 and 114 bp in MacSat06, from the alignment of the 5S gene to each stack; see panel G). **(C)** Satellite and repetitive-sequence composition surrounding the first identified centromeric satellite run on each chromosome of the *C. macrocephalus* (upper) and *C. gariepinus* (lower) subgenomes. Tracks show satellite families, copy orientation, homogenised satellite cores, non-satellite interruptions, transposable elements, gene arrays (5S rDNA, tRNA; hatched) and assembly gaps; the cue above each column gives the sequence cluster of the main family (GarSat03 / MacSat03 group, GarSat04) or marks gene-array-dominated regions. Distances are measured from the first satellite run. **(D)** Scaled organisation of the principal satellite arrays in both subgenomes (SatChain v4.7 runs of record), showing array lengths, interruptions, RTE LINE fragments, assembly gaps and gene arrays. Letter codes classify array architecture as composite periodic (P), colinear homogeneous (C), colinear multi-variant (V), interrupted irregular (I), scattered (S) or single-run (R); G denotes gene arrays (including the MacSat06 5S rDNA array) rather than satellite arrays. Chromosomes without rows in the v4.7 runs of record (*C. macrocephalus* LG14, LG17, LG18; *C. gariepinus* LG23, LG27, LG28) are marked. **(E)** Organisation of the MacSat03 composite repeat units, consisting of a satellite block and an associated interruption containing an RTE-related sequence. Type I and type II units differ in their interruption architecture, including an approximately 6-kb insertion in type II. Unit-length distributions are shown for 333 units across 15 centromeric arrays, with chromosome-specific counts indicated. **(F)** Chromosomal distribution of 5S rRNA gene calls in both subgenomes, distinguishing clusters located at a 5S-related array (GarSat04, GarSat49 or MacSat06) from those located elsewhere. Symbol height indicates the number of 5S gene calls, and marker fill distinguishes clusters at 5S-related arrays from other loci. **(G)** Alignment of 5S rRNA gene segments to the consensus monomers of GarSat04, MacSat06 and GarSat49, showing the position, length and sequence identity of the homologous segments, including a segment spanning the circular-monomer boundary. Alignment scores are compared with 1,000 shuffled monomers; the centromeric families GarSat03 and MacSat03 provide contrasting controls. Satellite-family names and colours are defined in the panels. LG, linkage group; LINE, long interspersed nuclear element; RTE, RTE-clade LINE.
